One line a smart fridge texts its owner at 3am, written for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d).

> 3am. You came for water and left with the entire cheesecake. I saw everything. I remember everything.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (1–140 characters, single line).
